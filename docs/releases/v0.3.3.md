The destructive buttons keep the outline look throughout.

- 0.3.2 filled them red once the confirmation was showing. The outline form reads better and
  matches the row action beside it, so the red is red **text on the theme's border** the
  whole way through — before and during the confirmation.
- What marks the step is the label (`彻底删除` → `确定删除`) and the warning line above it,
  not a slab of colour.
- The render check follows: the confirmation must be red **and** unfilled, so a later change
  cannot quietly reintroduce the fill.
