The destructive actions in the trash are red, in the theme's own colour

- **"Empty the trash"** and a row's **"Delete for good"** take the theme's destructive
  colour: red text on the button's outline, the same before and during the confirmation.
- What marks the confirmation is the label (`彻底删除` → `确定删除`) and a line of red text in
  the dialog body naming what will go — including the conversation's own title — rather than
  a change of fill.
- `Button` has no danger variant (only `primary`, `ghost`, `outline`, `toolbar`), so the red
  is read from the token the menu row's `danger` uses — `--dsw-alias-state-error-primary` —
  and follows the theme in light and dark rather than being written as a literal.
- Pinned by checks that assert the shape rather than the pixels: the confirm control's label
  is exactly `确定删除` and not the sentence, the warning carries the danger colour, and the
  confirmation must be red **and** unfilled.
